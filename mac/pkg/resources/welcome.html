Welcome to VibeHub

This installs VibeHub in /Applications and opens it once the install finishes.

VibeHub is a menu-bar app: no Dock icon, no window. It shows what you're coding in, right beside the notch. Press Start tracking in the app and a background tracker keeps your profile live, even when the app isn't open.

  • Menu bar item + a small panel beside the notch
  • A background tracker — only once you start it — that comes back after a reboot
  • Nothing else on this Mac is touched
