VibeHub just opened. Click Connect in Browser to link this Mac — no token to copy, no installer ever carries one.

Also installed: a vibehub-tracker command in your ~/.local/bin. It’s optional, and it removes itself if you trash the app. If your shell can’t find it, run this and reopen the terminal:
echo 'export PATH="$HOME/.local/bin:$PATH"' >> ~/.zshrc
(bash: use ~/.bash_profile instead — a login bash never reads ~/.bashrc.)
